## Decisions and mismatches found while writing the statements
1. **One length class.** The sieve runs on A = `piStar b N`: palindromes with exactly 2N+1 digits that are coprime to b³ − b, with x = b^{2N+1}.
   - Effect: TP's sup over y ≤ x drops out.
   - Effect: the audit's cylinder issue for Prop. W (shorter lengths, cut top block) does not arise.
   - Effect: infinitude is immediate, since different N give disjoint ranges.
2. **3^ω weights.** The usual trick of Cauchy–Schwarz plus a trivial bound for Σ 9^ω A_q fails for palindromes, because no divisor-sum bound over palindromes is available. The weights must instead be carried through M10's Fourier route:
   - After grouping, Σ_{r∣q≤D} 3^{ω(q)}/q ≤ 3^{ω(r)} r⁻¹ log³D.
   - The bound 3^{ω(r)} ≤ exp(C log r/log log r) is absorbed by the power saving for large r and by Col's exp(−σN/log r) for small r.
   - This is written into `Level.lean`. It is not yet reflected in PROOF_buniform.md §3, which states only the unweighted bound.
3. **Richert sums over A_p need ω·3^ω.** The Selberg remainders of the A_p, summed over p, need weights ω(q)·3^{ω(q)}. This costs one factor log x against the log³x saving, which is harmless. The hypothesis of `sieve_omega_le_five` is therefore kept with 3^ω weights.
4. **Fixed parameters.** θ = 6/25, z = x^{3/50}, y = x^{23/100}, λ = 8/5, squares controlled for x^{1/20} ≤ p ≤ x^{23/100}.
   - The LP check `omega_ok(1.6, 0.06, 0.23, 0.23)` holds.
   - λ = 8/5 rather than the maximal 1.6549 leaves a margin of 0.055 in the Ω bookkeeping.
   - Float values: crude W/XV = +0.414, JR +0.481.
5. **M14 is not stated yet.** The crude lower bound f_c(4) = 1 − ¼∫_3^∞(F_c − 1) uses F_c(v) = e^γ/∫_0^{v/2}ρ for all v, so it needs Dickman's ρ beyond [0, 2].
   - Upper-bound arguments only use ρ on [0, 2], where ρ = 1 or 1 − log u.
   - Option (i): replace F_c − 1 for large v by Brun's fundamental lemma, which gives an explicit decay. Then only ρ on [0, 2] is needed.
   - Option (ii): define ρ in Lean by its delay equation.
   - The choice is made together with the M13 lower-bound route, and it is the remaining S0 item. I recommend option (i).
